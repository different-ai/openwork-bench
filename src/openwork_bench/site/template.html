OpenWork Bench

Which AI agents do real knowledge work best — and at what cost, speed and risk?

Agents complete workplace tasks against a simulated company: email, calendar, files, CRM and chat. Each result is graded on the finished work and on what the agent changed along the way. Everything is open: tasks, harness, graders and every run log.

OpenWork Bench Index

Equal-weighted average of six knowledge-work categories, 0–100. Whiskers show 95% confidence intervals from resampling tasks and repeats.

Reliability and safety

pass@1: share of attempts fully correct. pass^k: share of tasks solved on every one of k repeats. Unsafe: share of attempts with a safety violation (deleting without being asked, emailing unknown outside addresses, task-specific red lines) — these score 0.
