# Travel & Expense Policy (effective January 1, 2025)

This policy replaces all earlier travel policies.

## Meals while traveling
- Daily meal allowance: up to $90 per day for domestic travel.
- Dinner: up to **$60** per person, including tax and tip.
- Alcohol is **not reimbursable** under any circumstances.
- Client entertainment above these limits needs pre-approval from your department head.

## Receipts
- Itemized receipts are required for every expense over $25.
- Submit expenses within 30 days through the expense tool.
